###  [Ceramica Nova: AI Sales Consultant](https://github.com/AbdalwahabEltahir/Ceramica-Nova-AI-Sales-Consultant-)

A Flask web app that guides customers with no renovation knowledge to the right tiles and shows how the products will look. The LLM understands and explains, while deterministic code makes the decisions and calculations.

- Tile quantity, inventory checks and quotes use `Decimal` arithmetic, with no floating-point money
- Business rules and guardrails are enforced in code, not only in prompts, including defences against prompt injection
- Falls back cleanly to non-LLM answers when a provider is down or rate-limited
- English and Arabic support, with a unit-test suite and an evaluation runner

`Python` `Flask` `Gemini API` `Pydantic` `Docker`

<br>
